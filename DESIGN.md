# ContentGuard Design System

See full specification in [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md).

ContentGuard follows a high-density, technical aesthetic built for Trust & Safety operators. It is monochrome, disciplined, and functional. Red is reserved exclusively for the `BLOCK` outcome and critical error alerts. Decisions are always conveyed through both typographic labels and geometric glyphs, never colour alone.

### Core Tokens
- `--black`: `#000000` (Canvas)
- `--surface`: `#111111` (Panels)
- `--surface-raised`: `#1A1A1A` (Insets / Hovers)
- `--border`: `#222222` (Subtle lines)
- `--border-visible`: `#333333` (Dividers / Borders)
- `--input-border`: `#666666` (Fields - WCAG 3.7:1)
- `--text-primary`: `#E8E8E8` (Body)
- `--text-display`: `#FFFFFF` (Headings)
- `--accent`: `#D71921` (BLOCK & Alerts)

### Typography
- Display Hero: `Doto` (72px)
- Interface & Body: `Space Grotesk`
- Technical & Data: `Space Mono`
